Privacy note

In plain language, because that's how we do business.

What we collect

When online receipt is enabled, your contact information and sourcing request are saved on our shared Taotao Trading backend with a unique inquiry reference and the Taotao Sourcing site identifier. Database inquiry records are retained for up to 12 months, subject to scheduled removal. A confirmed inquiry is queued for email notification and a restricted sales spreadsheet. Order records may have separate retention requirements. You can ask Katherine to access, correct or remove your inquiry.

Optional analytics and advertising measurement start only after you choose them. Browser records retain the first landing, site-specific visitor/session identifiers and up to 20 ad clicks for 90 days; up to 60 page and button events are kept for 30 days. This limited history may be attached to your inquiry. It can be incomplete and never proves your actual search query.

Advertising measurement, when allowed, links a recorded ad click with real sales stages sent to Google. Enhanced conversions, if enabled and permitted, use hashed contact identifiers. Personalization is disabled. Ordinary analytics events do not contain your contact details or requirement text. The two domains and the product catalogue do not automatically share visitor identity. Google and delivery providers may process information outside your country under their applicable terms.

Privacy choices last 180 days. Use “Privacy choices” in the footer to withdraw optional tracking and clear local journey records. Refusing tracking does not prevent submitting an inquiry. WhatsApp and email links open external services; clicks do not prove a message was sent.

How we use it

To answer your inquiry, quote products or services, and manage any project you engage us for. We may keep your details to continue the business relationship. We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Project confidentiality

Supplier names, prices and specifications you share with us during a sourcing project stay confidential. Likewise, reports we prepare for you belong to you. We never publish client information without written permission.

Your choices

Ask us any time to correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you — we will do it promptly.

Contact

Questions about this note: message Katherine on WhatsApp or via the inquiry form.
