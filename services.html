Sourcing & risk-control services

Choose a defined check for the supplier, site, product or order decision in front of you. Each assignment states the evidence requested, work performed and limits of the result.

🔍

Supplier Verification & Risk Screening

Before you send a deposit, we compare public registration information, supplier-provided documents and other available evidence against the company behind the quotation. This can identify inconsistencies and unanswered questions; it does not determine solvency or guarantee performance.

Business license & registration Export-evidence review Capability claims to verify Risk red flags

You receive: a written verification report — what was checked, what was found, what remains unknown, and our recommendation.

🏭

Factory Visits & Scoped Capability Reviews

Subject to location, supplier authorization and a written scope, we can visit a South China site and document dated observations relevant to your order—such as stated processes, equipment, storage, identification and traceability.

On-site photos & video Dated visit report Management meeting Capability review

You receive: a dated report that separates observations from supplier statements and records access limitations. A visit does not by itself prove ownership, future capacity, regulatory compliance or delivery performance.

📋

Inspection & QC Follow-up

Quality problems are often easier and less costly to address before shipment. We inspect against a checklist agreed with you in advance — first article, during production, or before loading — and report in plain language.

Agreed checklist Photo evidence Pass / fail summary Corrective follow-up

You receive: an inspection report with photo evidence and a pass/fail conclusion for the agreed sample, checks and criteria at that inspection. It is not a zero-defect guarantee, product certification or regulatory approval.

📦

Managed Sourcing

Your procurement office in South China: requirement analysis, RFQ to screened suppliers, sampling, price negotiation, production follow-up, inspection and shipping coordination — managed end to end in fluent English.

RFQ & negotiation Sample management Production follow-up Shipping coordination

You receive: one point of coordination, with update frequency and post-shipment support defined in the written project scope.
