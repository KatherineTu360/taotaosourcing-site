Sourcing & risk-control services

The checks a credit officer runs before lending money — applied to the factories that will build your product. Use any service on its own, or combine them into a managed sourcing package.

🔍

Supplier Verification & Risk Screening

Before you send a deposit, we check the company behind the quotation. Using a written checklist refined from over ten years of corporate credit work, we assess the observable evidence of a supplier's stability and capability.

Business license & registration Export track record Production capability Risk red flags

You receive: a written verification report — what was checked, what was found, what remains unknown, and our recommendation.

🏭

Factory Visits & Audits

A factory that photographs well on Alibaba may photograph very differently in person. We visit South China factories as part of normal client work and document what we actually see — production lines, equipment, working conditions, warehouse stock.

On-site photos & video Dated visit report Management meeting Capability review

You receive: a dated visit report with photo and video evidence, and an honest read on whether the factory can deliver your order.

📋

Inspection & QC Follow-up

Quality problems are cheap to fix before shipment and expensive after it. We inspect against a checklist agreed with you in advance — first article, during production, or before loading — and report in plain language.

Agreed checklist Photo evidence Pass / fail summary Corrective follow-up

You receive: an inspection report with photo evidence and a clear pass/fail conclusion against your criteria.

📦

Full Sourcing Outsourcing

Your procurement office in South China: requirement analysis, RFQ to screened suppliers, sampling, price negotiation, production follow-up, inspection and shipping coordination — managed end to end in fluent English.

RFQ & negotiation Sample management Production follow-up Shipping coordination

You receive: one point of accountability, weekly progress updates, and a partner who answers after the container sails.

The difference

Honest reporting — including bad news

  • ✓

    What we verified

    Documents seen, premises visited, certificates copied — with dates and evidence attached.

  • ~

    What no visitor can confirm from outside — stated plainly, with the risk it creates for your order.

    Proceed, proceed with safeguards, or walk away — with the reasoning in writing.
