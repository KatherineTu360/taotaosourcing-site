TDA and TDB Steel Truss Decking System

Factory-welded reinforcement trusses combined with a galvanized profiled-steel bottom sheet for composite floor construction.

TDA / TDB

Product overview

The TDA/TDB system combines factory-produced reinforcement trusses with a galvanized profiled-steel bottom form. The catalogue provides material categories, truss dimensions and weld-point shear tables for preliminary review. Structural calculations, steel certificates, welding procedure qualification and project-specific shop drawings remain required before procurement.

  • Factory-welded truss and bottom-sheet assembly
  • TDA and TDB system forms
  • Catalogue dimensional and weld-point tables
  • Applicable to composite floor construction
