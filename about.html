The story

From credit officer to sourcing partner

For more than ten years, Katherine Tu worked at a state-owned bank in China, spending years in corporate lending — assessing the financial health, production capability and operational risks of manufacturing companies deciding whether they could be trusted with a loan.

Her earlier career included export work in stainless-steel products, so she had seen the factory floor and the buyer's side of the table. But it was banking that taught her the skill most sourcing intermediaries never develop: telling a stable manufacturer from a fragile one, before it's obvious.

In 2023 she founded Zhaoqing Taotao Import and Export Trading Co., Ltd. in Zhaoqing, Guangdong — in the middle of South China's manufacturing belt — to bring that same risk discipline to overseas buyers purchasing helmets, industrial products and custom-sourced goods from China.

She holds a Master of Translation and Interpreting in English from Guangxi University and is co-author of the bestselling book Super Learner. So when she reports on your supplier, you get precise, unfiltered English — not a translation of what a salesperson wanted you to hear.
