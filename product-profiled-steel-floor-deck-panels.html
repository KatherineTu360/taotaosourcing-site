Product overview

The catalogue provides a dense range of profiled floor decks for preliminary structural comparison. Each profile has different effective width, feed width, thickness and section properties, so the model must be selected against span, wet-concrete and construction loads. A project order requires structural confirmation, steel grade, coating, tolerances and approved shop drawings.

  • Open and closed deck profiles
  • Effective widths from 340 to 970 mm across the listed range
  • Model-specific thickness options
  • Catalogue section inertia and resistance tables
