Sending Product Samples from China? Check the Customs Invoice Before Pickup

By Katherine Tu · Published November 18, 2026 · 4 min read

Real photograph from a business visit, used as product context only. It does not establish the contents or customs status of any shipment.

Katherine from Taotao Trading

You have approved a presentation-box sample and the supplier is ready to book a courier. The draft paperwork says only “samples.” Before authorizing pickup, can you connect each physical item to a clear description and a supported declared value?

Why this small decision matters

The sample fee, freight charge and value declared for customs serve different purposes. A free sample is still a physical item. Calling it a sample does not, by itself, establish the right customs value, invoice type or duty treatment. An unclear description can leave the receiver answering questions after the parcel has already left China.

DHL Express’s public document guidance says the paperwork depends on the goods, value, destination, parties and service. It lists the reason for export separately from commodity details such as description, origin, quantity and value. Treat this as a reason to verify the actual shipment with the carrier or broker, not a universal invoice template for every country.

Request one matched packet before collection

Ask for the draft customs invoice, itemized packing list and clear photographs of the actual contents. Keep them under the same shipment reference. Use this Taotao check before giving the courier-booking go-ahead:

CheckEvidence to reconcile
What is inside?Each sample type, quantity, material and intended use; avoid “accessories” or “samples” as the entire description.
Why is it being sent?The actual purpose: evaluation, sale, return or another confirmed reason. Do not label a paid purchase as a gift to reduce charges.
How was the value determined?The supplier’s value basis and currency, with sample, freight and other charges identified. Ask the carrier or broker how the destination treats a free or discounted sample.
Who receives and clears it?Confirmed receiver details and any importer information the carrier requests, exchanged privately through the approved channel.
Does the parcel match?Contents, quantities, parcel count and weights consistent across the packing list, invoice and booking.

How to verify the packet

Read the invoice beside the item photographs and sample agreement. Resolve differences before dispatch. If the sample includes removable inserts or additional components, establish whether these require separate commodity detail rather than silently grouping everything together. Classification and value questions belong with the responsible shipper and a competent customs adviser; a product photograph is not enough to settle them.

My banking background informs this consistency check: compare the purpose of a payment with the documents supporting it, and identify what is still missing. It does not provide access to private banking information or a guarantee of clearance.

Red flags and limitations

Pause if the supplier proposes an arbitrary token value, changes the description to avoid scrutiny, or cannot explain which items are included. A commercial or pro forma invoice accepted on one route may not be appropriate on another. No duty-free status or delivery date is established by this checklist.

Continue / Pause / Escalate

  • Continue: the contents and paperwork agree, the value basis is documented and the carrier’s route-specific requirements have been checked.
  • Pause: the packing list, pictures or invoice describe different goods, quantities or purposes.
  • Escalate: valuation, classification, restricted goods or importer responsibilities remain unresolved; ask the carrier or customs broker before pickup.

Product context: Jewelry packaging and display products. Exact materials, sample prices and shipping terms remain subject to confirmation.

Send a redacted sample item list through our inquiry page for a scoped consistency review. Remove buyer names, prices, bank details, addresses and personal contacts from the review copy; share only authorized material.
