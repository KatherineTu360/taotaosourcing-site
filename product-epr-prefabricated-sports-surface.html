EPR Prefabricated Sports Surface Tiles

Square prefabricated sports-surface tiles listed in 50 × 50 cm at 10 mm and 61 × 61 cm at 13 mm.

50 × 50 cm / 61 × 61 cm

Product overview

The EPR surface is a prefabricated tile system based on modified EPDM material. The catalogue shows two square formats, patterned top faces, drainage channels, interlocking edges, fixing holes and a central vent. Buyers should confirm colour, substrate, edge details, installation method and obtain the cited GB 36246-2018 test report before specifying compliance.

  • Two documented tile sizes
  • Patterned top surface
  • Drainage channels and central vent shown
  • Interlocking edge and fixing holes shown
