HB-X Up-and-Down Foldable LED Poster Display

A 646 × 2050 mm foldable poster-display series with GOB and seamless-splicing functions listed in the supplier worksheet.

HB-X2.5 / HB-X1.86 / HB-X1.53 / HB-X1.25

Product overview

The HB-X series is designed for buyers who need a tall LED poster that can be folded for transport and deployed without a fixed installation. The listed models share the same overall size and 45 kg weight, while pixel pitch, resolution and power differ. Confirm the folding mechanism, packing dimensions and control configuration against the selected model before ordering.

  • Up-and-down folding format
  • Seamless multi-unit splicing listed
  • GOB technology listed
  • Four indoor pixel-pitch options
