C-Section Steel Purlins

A ten-size C-section purlin range with catalogue section dimensions, thickness options and configurable hole spacing.

Product overview

The C-section range covers nominal depths from 80 to 300 mm for roof and wall secondary framing. The supplier table lists flange, lip and thickness options, with hole spacing adjustable on most sizes. Buyers should provide structural drawings, steel grade, coating, cut lengths and hole pattern rather than ordering from the section code alone.

  • Nominal depths from 80 to 300 mm
  • Multiple thickness options by section
  • Adjustable hole spacing on most models
  • Project-specific cutting and punching to be confirmed
