Product overview

This grouped envelope range includes profiled roof and wall sheets, concealed-fix and standing-seam formats, curved panels, rock-wool/foam/polyurethane sandwich panels and FRP/PC skylights. The catalogue gives many profile widths and thickness options, but thermal, fire, coating and span requirements depend on the exact assembly. Buyers should send elevations, panel schedule and destination-standard requirements.

  • Roof, wall and liner profiles
  • Concealed-fix and standing-seam options
  • Rock-wool, foam and polyurethane sandwich-panel options
  • FRP/PC skylight and curved-panel options
