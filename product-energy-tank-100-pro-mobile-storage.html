Energy Tank 100 Pro

Product overview

Energy Tank 100 Pro is presented as a mobile storage and bidirectional charging unit for parking areas, logistics sites and temporary-power projects. Its catalogue table lists 104 kWh capacity, 120 kW V2E output and a 105 kW V2G interface. Buyers should confirm cell traceability, applicable grid-code requirements, charging connector standards and full certification files for the destination market.

  • Mobile storage and charging format
  • LFP battery chemistry listed
  • V2E and V2G interfaces listed
  • Optional 20 kW V2L function
