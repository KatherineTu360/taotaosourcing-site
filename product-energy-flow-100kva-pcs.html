Product overview

Energy Flow is a smaller power-conversion option for modular storage and distributed-energy installations. Its catalogue table lists a single 150 A DC channel, selectable AC arrangements and common industrial communications. Buyers should provide the grid connection, battery-voltage window and operating environment so the supplier can confirm a compatible configuration.
